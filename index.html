Accuracy without interpretability is incomplete science — I build models that explain themselves.

I am Saifuddin Sagor, a Computer Science & Engineering graduate from Daffodil International University (DIU), Bangladesh. Currently, I work as a Research Assistant at DIU, a Lead Researcher at SynthaNano Lab, and a Research Associate at Nexcure Lab. My research focuses on Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, and Natural Language Processing (NLP), with a strong commitment to building reliable, transparent, and deployment-ready AI systems. I work extensively with modern deep learning architectures—including Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), Vision Transformers (ViTs), Mamba, and Graph Neural Networks (GNNs)—integrated with Explainable AI (XAI) to solve real-world challenges in precision agriculture and healthcare diagnostics. My research has been published in premier scientific venues, including journals by Nature Portfolio and Elsevier, conference proceedings with IEEE and Springer, a book chapter with Taylor & Francis, and preprints on arXiv. Dedicated to open science, I actively collect real-world raw data from the field and curate multiple benchmark datasets published on Mendeley Data to support reproducible research globally. Beyond research, I also serve the scientific community as an invited international journal peer reviewer and academic mentor.

Key Research Highlights:

  • Publications: Peer-reviewed articles in Springer Nature (Sci Rep, Q1), Elsevier (Data in Brief, Q1), and IEEE/Springer.
  • Open Science: Real-world raw data collection and multiple benchmark datasets published on Mendeley Data.
  • Peer Reviewer: Invited reviewer for Measurement: Food (Elsevier) and EJSRR.
  • Affiliations & Leadership: Researcher at DIU, SynthaNano Lab, Nexcure Lab; former Lead at DIU Cyber Security Club.

🔬 Research Interests & Focus Domains

🌿 Agricultural AI & Plant Disease Detection

Deep learning models for automatic detection and classification of plant diseases from leaf images, targeting real-world deployment in South Asian agriculture.

👁 Computer Vision & Hybrid Architectures

Hybrid CNN-ViT frameworks, transfer learning, image classification, and CycleGAN-based data augmentation for imbalanced agricultural datasets.

🧠 Explainable AI (XAI)

LIME and SHAP-based model interpretability techniques to make deep learning decisions transparent and trustworthy for clinicians and policymakers.

🔬 Medical Image Analysis

Deep learning-based analysis of clinical medical images including sperm morphology (CSAD dataset), cell classification, and disease biomarker detection — bridging AI research with healthcare applications.

💬 NLP & Mental Health AI

Transformer models, LoRA fine-tuning, and low-resource NLP for mental health detection from Bangla and English social media text.

🎓 Undergraduate Thesis

LXViT: A Hybrid Hierarchical Xception-ViT Framework for Lemon Leaf Disease Classification with Multi-Method Explainable AI Integration

Undergraduate thesis combining Xception (deep CNN) feature extraction with Vision Transformer (ViT) hierarchical attention mechanisms for lemon leaf disease classification. Validated using both LIME and SHAP post-hoc explainability frameworks for real-world trust and transparency. Supervised by MD Masum Billah (Asst. Prof., DIU).
